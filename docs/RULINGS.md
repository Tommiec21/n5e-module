# Approved rules and scope

- Concentration DC is max(15 + combined maintained jutsu ranks, half damage); the core chapter's base 12 does not apply.
- Either HP or Chakra damage can trigger concentration checks; ordinary resource spending is not damage.
- Addition/subtraction precedes multiplication/division; explicitly group dice formulas instead of altering global Foundry Roll semantics.
- Opposing half/double damage sources cancel; duplicate sources must not silently compound.
- Static DR and rolled DR follow the same allocation rules, with an independent budget for HP and Chakra pools.
- Within one pool, the damaged creature chooses damage-type allocation and remaining DR passes to the next type.
- Clones are ordinary NPCs, Combination casting is excluded, and Clash is opposed relevant skill rolls.
- Automated reactions are limited to successful hit and damage-roll timing; other timing is manual and nested chains are deferred.
- Clan identity uses native species/race infrastructure; removed Clan Feats and redesigned tables are not approved imports.
