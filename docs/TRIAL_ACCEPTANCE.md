Goal: prove that native Items/activities can connect payment, rolls, target saves, damage/conditions and maintained-cast records without rebuilding a whole system.

Use game.modules.get("n5e-module").api.trial.importItems(actor) on an owned test actor; this is an opt-in in-game action and was not executed against the user’s installation.

Set the actor’s Chakra resources explicitly, then select target tokens and use one of the native trial activities; provide the full attack/DC/unarmed inputs and confirm source eligibility until Batches08/10 deliver general calculation/validation.

Check Chakra Bolt’s attack/damage, the three Adept Healing modes, and D-rank Butterfly Kick’s separate normal and Finisher paths.

Use Bane and Arms of Buddha as two maintained jutsu, and Fracturing Splinter as the source-reviewed mixed HP/Chakra save case.

GM/owners resolve hit eligibility and target save cards, then apply source-linked damage/outcomes; no third-party automation or remote owner bypass is introduced.

Native spell level0 is a carrier only, not a mapping of E–S ranks to spell slots, and native consumption is empty because the bridge owns one approved Chakra payment.

Native Items contain full source/provenance and separate activities, but the trial is not the968-entry native compendium conversion scheduled for Batch14.
